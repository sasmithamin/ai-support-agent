Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Visit our signup page, enter your email and create a password. You'll receive a verification email to activate your account.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required.

Q: Can I change my plan later?
A: Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime from the billing settings.

Q: Do you offer refunds?
A: We have a 30-day money-back guarantee for all new subscriptions.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: You can reach us via:
- Email: support@example.com
- Live chat (available 24/7 in the app)
- Phone: 1-800-SUPPORT

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for enterprise customers.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes, we use 256-bit SSL encryption and are SOC 2 Type II certified.

Q: Can I invite team members?
